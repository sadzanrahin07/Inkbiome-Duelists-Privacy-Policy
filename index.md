Sadzan Rahin ("we," "our," or "us") provides **Inkbiome Duelists** (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play the Game, use its multiplayer features, or contact us.

The Game offers offline modes, same-Wi-Fi duels, and internet friend rooms powered by Epic Online Services (EOS). You do not need to enter your name, email address, telephone number, payment information, or an Epic account password to use the current gameplay features. Internet multiplayer nevertheless uses technical identifiers and network information; it is not anonymous in the sense of processing no personal data.

## 1. Information stored on your device

The Game stores a local profile containing information such as:

- Wins, losses, draws, Arcade completions, and tutorial completion.
- Selected fighter and difficulty preferences.
- Sound, screen shake, touch controls, control size, handedness, reduced effects, and vibration preferences.
- A local launch counter used by the Game's interface and feature logic.

We do not currently provide publisher-operated cloud saves or cross-device progress synchronization. Match data is exchanged with another player during multiplayer, but the local profile is not uploaded as a cloud-save file by the Game.

Your operating system or backup provider may back up app data according to your device settings. Such backups are managed by that provider.

## 2. Internet multiplayer and Epic Online Services

When you choose to create or join an internet friend room, the Game connects to EOS to authenticate the installation, discover a room, and connect the players.

Information used for these features includes:

- An EOS installation/device identity and Product User ID.
- Room codes, lobby identifiers, membership, and connection status.
- Fighter and dojo selections, Ready status, and match-session information.
- Gameplay inputs and state, such as movement, attacks, damage, stocks, ink coverage, match timing, and results.
- Network and technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as IP addresses, device/platform information, application version, and connection diagnostics.

The current Game uses EOS Connect Device ID authentication. It does not require a player-facing "Sign in with Epic" account login. An identifier is created or reused for the installation; this does not provide account-linked progress recovery across devices.

One player's device hosts the authoritative fight. Gameplay information is exchanged with the other player's device using EOS peer-to-peer networking, with relay support when needed. Network addressing information may be processed by Epic and, depending on the connection route, by the other participant's device. A room code should be shared only with someone you intend to play with.

We use these data to establish and maintain matches, synchronize gameplay, handle results and rematches, and diagnose connection problems. The current Game does not offer public player chat, voice chat, or a public player-profile directory.

Epic's processing is also subject to [Epic Games' Privacy Policy](https://legal.epicgames.com/epicgames/privacy-policy).

## 3. Same-Wi-Fi multiplayer

Same-Wi-Fi mode uses local-network discovery and a direct connection to the hosting device. Nearby devices running the Game may receive room advertisements, including the room name, local host address, game version, availability, and connection port.

Once connected, the devices exchange lobby settings, controls, gameplay state, and results. Do not use a room name containing personal or sensitive information. Local-network discovery does not require access to your address book or precise GPS location.

## 4. Google Play Games and Google services

The Android version includes Google Play Games Services. The Game checks the connection status and offers a sign-in/retry option. If Google Play Games authenticates you, the Game can request your current player information and display your Google Play Games player name.

Google may process account, device, authentication, and service information to provide its services. The Game does not receive your Google account password. Google Play Games authentication is separate from EOS multiplayer, and offline gameplay does not require a successful Google Play Games connection.

Google Play distribution and device services may also process information under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Advertising and analytics

The current version covered by this policy does **not** have an active third-party advertising provider or advertising SDK integrated into its ad interface. We do not currently request or display advertisements through that interface.

The Game does not currently include a separate publisher-operated analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Platform providers and integrated services may process technical diagnostics under their own policies. If you choose to report a problem, we may ask you to send relevant diagnostic information.

If we introduce advertising, additional analytics, account linking, or other materially different data processing, we will update this policy and provide any disclosures, choices, or consent mechanisms required before that processing begins.

## 6. Support and privacy correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include, such as a problem description, device model, screenshot, or diagnostic log. We use this information to respond, investigate problems, and handle privacy requests.

Please do not send passwords, private signing keys, payment details, government identification, or other sensitive information. Your email provider and ours process correspondence under their own terms and privacy policies.

## 7. How information is used and shared

Information is used to:

- Save local preferences and records.
- Authenticate service connections and provide multiplayer rooms.
- Synchronize fights and deliver results to both players.
- Display Google Play Games connection information when available.
- Maintain service security, investigate faults, and respond to support requests.
- Meet applicable legal obligations.

Information needed for multiplayer is shared with the other participant and the relevant service providers as described above. We do not sell your personal information or upload your local gameplay profile to an advertising network.

We may disclose information under our control when required by applicable law or a valid legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect users and service security. Epic, Google, hosting providers, and email providers may process information in other countries; their policies describe their handling of international transfers.

## 8. Retention and deletion

**Local game data:** You can remove the local profile by clearing the Game's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the Game. This can permanently remove your local progress. Operating-system backups may retain copies according to your backup settings.

**Multiplayer data and identity:** Room/session information is used while connecting and playing. The Game attempts to leave or destroy its lobby when a player exits normally. Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not itself guarantee deletion of records, identifiers, logs, or backups held by Epic or Google. Those providers apply their own retention practices.

**Support correspondence:** We retain correspondence only as reasonably needed to resolve your request, follow up on a problem, maintain necessary security records, or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion by contacting us.

For requests concerning data we control, email [sadzanrahin2001@gmail.com](mailto:sadzanrahin2001@gmail.com). We will review the request and explain any verification or retention requirements. For information controlled by Epic or Google, their privacy policies provide additional request channels. We cannot promise to erase information independently controlled by another provider.
